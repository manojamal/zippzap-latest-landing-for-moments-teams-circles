What every ZippZap includes

Whichever ZippZap you choose, these come with it.

Every kind of wish

Video, voice, photo or written. Everyone sends theirs from their own phone.

One link for everyone

Share it on WhatsApp, KingsChat, email or anywhere your people are.

Revealed on the day

The film stays a surprise until the day you choose.

Any screen

Plays on a phone, a TV or the big screen.

Yours to keep

Download the film and share it again whenever you like.

Pay your way

Card, bank transfer, KingsPay or Espees.

How ZippZap works

The same three steps, whoever you are celebrating.

  1. 1

    Start a celebration

    Pick the occasion and the day the film is revealed.

  2. 2

    Invite everyone

    Share one link. Everyone adds a video, voice note, photo or message from their phone.

  3. 3

    Reveal one film

    Play it on the day, on a phone, a TV or the big screen.
